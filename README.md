📊 Diwali Sales Analysis – Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

---

📌 Project Overview

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a Diwali Sales dataset to understand customer purchasing behavior, revenue trends, and product performance across different states and demographics.

The objective is to generate actionable business insights that can help businesses improve marketing strategies, customer targeting, and inventory planning during festive seasons.

---

🛠 Tools & Libraries Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

---

🔍 Key Steps Performed

- Data cleaning and preprocessing
- Handling missing values and removing irrelevant columns
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Customer segmentation analysis (age, gender, occupation, and state)
- Identification of top-performing product categories and products
- Revenue and sales trend visualization

---

📊 Key Insights

- Married women aged 26–35 contribute significantly to overall sales
- Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka generate the highest revenue
- IT, Healthcare, and Aviation professionals show strong purchasing behavior
- Clothing and Electronics categories perform exceptionally well
- Certain product IDs dominate revenue generation

---

📌 Conclusion

The analysis highlights high-value customer segments and top-performing product categories during the Diwali season.

These insights can help businesses:

- Target profitable customer segments
- Optimize inventory management
- Improve festive marketing campaigns
- Increase overall sales performance
